# ContextSDK

> ContextSDK is an on-device context intelligence layer for mobile apps. It processes 300+ real-world device signals (accelerometer, gyroscope, motion, screen state, ambient signals) through on-device ML to tell an app what the user is doing in the moment — commuting, relaxing, working, active — within seconds of app launch, with no PII collected. Products: ContextDecision (intelligent timing for in-app actions) and ContextPush (context-aware push delivery). SOC 2 Type 2 certified and GDPR compliant; iOS GA with Flutter/React Native/Unity support and Android in beta.
